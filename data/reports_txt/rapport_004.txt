During the morning inspection, the compressor C-102 showed
higher vibration than usual. The maintenance team stopped the
unit and inspected the motor side. The bearing was found worn
and was replaced. After restarting, vibration levels were
acceptable.

At the same time, pump P-204 was showing a pressure drop.
Inspection revealed a small leakage around the mechanical seal.
The seal was replaced and the pump was tested for 30 minutes.
No further leakage was observed.

The heat exchanger E-301 was also inspected because the outlet
temperature was higher than normal. Deposits were found on the
heat transfer surfaces. Cleaning was carried out and the
temperature returned to normal.
